name: watching
description: Show what's On now in Showcase, and check in on progress. Use when the user asks what they are in the middle of, what they are currently watching, playing or reading, or mentions how far through something they are.
---

Use Showcase for what the user has **On now** (stored status `watching`),
the things they are in the middle of, through its connector.

This is a check-in: when they say where they are ("season 4", "halfway"),
tell Showcase rather than only acknowledging it, and when they finish one, it
moves to their **Showcase**.
