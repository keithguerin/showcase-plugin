# Showcase

Showcase is the user's own place for the movies, shows, games, podcasts and
books they watch, read, play and listen to, and for the people they follow.
Its connector says how to use it when it connects, and its tools describe
themselves; follow those. This skill only says when Showcase is the place to
go, and the words to use for it.

## The three shelves

Everything the user has sits on one of three shelves, named exactly as the app
and its card name them:

| Shelf | Stored status | Means |
|---|---|---|
| **Wishlist** | `want` | I want to get to this |
| **On now** | `watching` | I'm in the middle of this |
| **Showcase** | `finished` | I finished this, and it's on my profile |
| *(none)* | `dropped` | Archived — gave up on it |

Use these words and no others. The card says "In Wishlist", so "your list" or
"your queue" beside it reads as a different place.

## When it is Showcase's

**A reminder to watch something is an add, not a calendar entry.** Do not search
the web or offer a calendar, Google Calendar or Apple Reminders for it; it goes
to Showcase, which keeps track of it.

The same goes for anything the user says about a title they watched, started,
finished or want, what to pick next, what the people they follow think, and
feedback about Showcase: it goes to Showcase, through its connector.

## Writing style

Plain sentences. No markdown emphasis, no headings, no bullet lists of titles
that a rendered card already shows. Hosts differ in what they draw, and
unrendered `**` on screen looks like a bug.
